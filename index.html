    INDONESIA IN THREE NOTES

    Sacred spaces

    Wear respectful coverings at temples, heed local signs and follow guides during ceremonies. Don't step on small offerings set on the ground.

    Wild means wild

    Give macaques, Komodo dragons and marine animals space. Keep food secured, follow rangers and avoid standing on coral.

    Small moments matter

    Carry small IDR notes, ask before photographing people, use reusable water bottles where safe, and enjoy the slower pace.

    Useful safety reminders

    On the water

    Follow boat crew and ranger instructions; wear the appropriate life jacket. Do not attempt to swim or snorkel in unsafe current conditions.

    In Komodo

    Never approach or feed dragons. Stay with your ranger and on the designated path; wild animals are unpredictable.

    Temples & villages

    Keep valuables secure and follow local guidance. Carry water and sun protection and respect ceremonial spaces.

    Health & medications

    Keep necessary medications and key documents with you, not only in checked luggage. Each traveller should use their own insurance emergency contact.
